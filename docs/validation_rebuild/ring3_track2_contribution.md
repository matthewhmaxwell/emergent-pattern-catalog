## Honest corrections the gates force on our own write-up
1. **DROP "closed set" / "completeness."** Unfalsifiable at n≈14 probes — an overclaim. The defensible statement
   is: *minimal-sufficient mechanisms are densely catalogued, so a toy-scale search is structurally biased toward
   rediscovering known primitives.* (The `ring3_synthesis.md` and cascade docs used "closed/complete" language;
   this is now corrected to "densely catalogued / structurally biased toward rediscovery.")
2. **Bound the minimal-mechanism finding to toy scale.** It is in direct tension with open-ended-evolution /
   novelty search (Lehman & Stanley 2011: you *escape* the minimal basis by scaling complexity). This *vindicates*
   the earlier retraction of the "low-odds at scale" claim — keep that retraction.
3. **The minimal-mechanism mechanism is NOT new.** It is simplicity bias (Shah 2020) + shortcut learning
   (Geirhos 2020) + specification gaming (Krakovna) + emergent-comms least-effort ("a code *sufficient* to solve
   the task," Chaabouni/Lazaridou/Baroni 2020) + Kirby's compression–expressivity tradeoff for the
   compositionality part. Present it as a *consolidation to the multi-agent-competency setting*, not a discovery.
4. **The coordination mechanisms are all textbook** (Schelling focal points; Crawford-Sobel cheap talk; referential
   games; distributed→compositional). Only the *unified ladder + symmetry-gated non-use* is thin.

## The recommended contribution (honest, defensible, low-effort)
**A methods / rigor paper on the interventional self-debunking competency instrument**, with the 21-competency
corpus + the coordination cascade as its worked demonstration.

- **Why this one:** it is the only candidate that gated *genuinely under-named* AND is *already fully evidenced*
  by work we've done — no new experiment required. The pieces (ablation, skeptical prior, intervention>probing,
  domain discriminators, fair baselines) are all conceded as prior art; the contribution is the **named, reflexive
  composite** + the **catalogued-vs-novel output axis**, positioned as the interventional successor to
  Ronald/Sipper/Capcarrère's 1999 "Design, Observation, Surprise!" emergence test (replace observer-*surprise*,
  which over-fires, with *survives-a-collapse-ablation*).
- **The killer demonstration:** an instrument that debunks *its own operator's* ~9 documented over-claims (broken
  memoryless baselines; reward-design artifacts; focal-point escapes; the swarm eval-time-ablation confound; the
  ToM 0.6-bar mislabel). A rigor instrument whose headline evidence is *catching the people using it* is exactly
  the credibility a methods paper needs.
- **Nearest neighbors to differentiate (related-work table):** Ronald/Sipper/Capcarrère 1999 (surprise-based, not
  interventional); Levin (perturbation-defines-competency, but tuned to *reveal*, not to catch over-claims —
  ours is the skeptical inversion); Schaeffer et al. 2023 (metric-mirage, but scaling-curve-specific); Lowe &
  Foerster 2019 (comms-specific discriminators); Hamilton et al. 2022 (eval-time ablation overstates dependence —
  our fair-baseline refinement); causal-interpretability (intervention on *internals*, ours on *behaviour*).
- **Venue:** ALife / *Artificial Life* (owns the emergence-test lineage) → then TMLR / a rigor-in-emergence venue.

## Secondary contribution (bundle into the same paper as a proposed measure)
**Shortcut-cascade depth** — genuinely under-named as a graded measure (quantifies Gardner et al. 2021
"Competency Problems," realized via AFLite/ANLI-style iterative shortcut closure). Present it as a *proposed
measure*, NOT a validated one, and lead with the well-definedness caveats: it must be defined relative to a fixed
learner class + a canonical shortcut enumeration; shown non-degenerate (each closure forecloses a *distinct*
mechanism — the exact "inflate the count" failure the instrument already polices); and correlated with an
independent hardness axis (e.g. minimal recurrent state/bits, sample complexity). ToM #20's six-shortcut cascade
is the illustrative example.

## What the other two need (future work, if pursued)
- **Cost-hierarchy** → one environment with a continuously *dialable* information structure (symmetric →
  single-source → distributed), showing agents recruit exactly the next-cheapest tier at each threshold, with an
  *available-but-declined channel* under symmetry. Our scramble/blind/focal-removal apparatus is built for this;
  it is the single missing experiment.
- **Temporal-scale axis** → genuine across-generation experiments (iterated learning; evolutionary outer loop) so
  the "across-generations" row is demonstrated not asserted; differentiate sharply from Watson & Szathmáry's
  "same mechanism across timescales." Strongest *combined* with cascade-depth: depth measured within each scale,
  orderings claimed invariant across scales.

## Bottom line
The search produced **0 new-to-science competencies** (honest, and now *explained*, not merely observed). The
genuine *contribution to the field* is not a competency but a **method**: a reflexive, interventional instrument
for honestly gating emergence-competency claims — demonstrated by an unusually credible corpus in which it
repeatedly caught its own operator's over-claims. Everything else is either textbook (the mechanisms), a
consolidation (minimal-sufficiency in multi-agent settings), or a proposed-but-unvalidated measure
(cascade-depth, temporal-scale). That is the honest, publishable shape.
