## Prioritized build queue

### [HIGH] Topological defect census / winding-number singularities
- **Discriminator:** Winding number (line integral of grad(angle)/2pi, or /pi for nematic head-tail) around each plaquette -> defect DENSITY + NET topological charge + opposite-charge pair-correlation/binding length. The discriminator is the SIGNED defect inventory and pair-binding length, NOT net order and NOT total |gradient| (both of which average defects to zero). Specifically report n_defect and net_charge separately: a balanced +-1/2 sea has high n_defect, ~0 net_charge, ~0 global nematic order - the exact signature the current battery is blind to.
- **Substrate:** continuous director/orientation field; oscillator phase lattice (XY); positions->coarse-grained director
- **Validation:** Positive control: XY model below vs above BKT (bound vs unbound vortex pairs) and an active-nematic field with +-1/2 defects (defect density should rise across the transition while global nematic S stays low). Null: an ordered single-domain nematic (zero defects) and iid-random angle field (defects present but pair-correlation = surrogate-shuffled, i.e. no binding structure). Earns place if it fires on the defect-laden-but-globally-disordered case where polar/nematic/vorticity all read ~0.
- **Risk/confound:** Discretization/noise spawns spurious half-quantized windings on near-uniform fields; needs a coherence/amplitude gate at cores (only count where local order magnitude collapses) and a shuffle surrogate to set the false-defect floor. Confound: distinguishing genuine cores from numerical aliasing at sharp gradients.

### [HIGH] Pattern symmetry / lattice class (angular structure of S(k))
- **Discriminator:** At the dominant |k| ring, take the AZIMUTHAL profile S(theta||k|=k*) and count discrete peaks: 2-fold=stripes, 4=square, 6=hexagonal, continuous=labyrinth/isotropic. Discriminator = azimuthal peak COUNT + angular concentration (orientational order of the pattern), plus a chirality index from asymmetry of the complex S(k) phase / curl of local pattern orientation. This is a NEW use of the already-computed S(k) - it reads the angle, not just |k_peak| (a shared absolute the current lens already has).
- **Substrate:** continuous 2D field; particle positions (via S(k)); phase field
- **Validation:** Positive control: reaction-diffusion sweep producing spots(hex)->stripes(2-fold)->labyrinth(ring) - the azimuthal peak count must track the morphology transition. Null: isotropic Gaussian-random field with a ring spectrum (continuous azimuth, no discrete peaks) and a single-crystal lattice (sharp discrete peaks but trivially ordered, distinguished from emergent selection by context). Earns place: cleanly separates 3 patterns the current S(k)-magnitude lens calls identical.
- **Risk/confound:** Low-amplitude / weakly anisotropic patterns give noisy azimuthal peaks; finite-box k-grid quantizes angle coarsely at small |k|. Needs significance test of azimuthal peaks vs an angularly-shuffled-phase surrogate of the same radial spectrum.

### [HIGH] Collective velocity-field order from bare positions (mill/polar/gas + scale-free velocity correlation)
- **Discriminator:** Form per-particle velocities by frame differencing, then report THREE separable scalars: global polarization |mean v_hat|, angular momentum about COM (mill detector), and the connected velocity-FLUCTUATION correlation length xi from C(r)=<dv_i.dv_j>. Discriminator that earns novelty = whether xi SCALES WITH SYSTEM SIZE L (scale-free flocking criticality), not the magnitude of order. Polar and mill are split by the polarization-vs-angular-momentum contrast; gas is split by xi->0.
- **Substrate:** particle positions (Nx2) across frames -> finite-difference velocities
- **Validation:** Positive control: Vicsek in polar phase (high polarization, xi~L), a milling ring (low polarization, high angular momentum), and disordered swarm (both low, xi small). Null: ideal gas / Brownian particles (no velocity correlation beyond Theiler window). Scale-free test: run 2-3 system sizes, confirm xi/L ~ const only in the critical flock. Earns place: distinguishes mill/polar/gas which share identical S(k) and positions-clustering.
- **Risk/confound:** Velocity from finite differencing is noisy at high temporal sampling / low displacement; needs frame-gap tuning. Confound: a globally translating gas (uniform drift) inflates raw polarization - must subtract mean velocity for the fluctuation correlation (the dv, not v).

### [HIGH] Metastability / regime-switching / change-points (incl. critical slowing down)
- **Discriminator:** Two coupled discriminators on the macrostate TIME SERIES: (1) HMM/GMM on a low-dim embedding -> number of well-separated long-DWELL macrostates + dwell-time distribution + transition-matrix sparsity (distinguishes unimodal-stationary from K>1 switching that share spectral/complexity/tail signatures); (2) sliding-window lag-1 autocorrelation and variance with Kendall-tau trend = critical-slowing-down early-warning. Discriminator = MODE COUNT + dwell heavy-tail + rising-AR(1) TREND, never the static variance level (a shared absolute).
- **Substrate:** any -> coarse-grained low-dim state vector / order-parameter time series
- **Validation:** Positive control: bistable double-well with noise (2 modes, exponential-ish dwell, flips) and a system driven slowly toward a saddle-node (AR(1)->1, variance up while mean flat). Null: unimodal Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (1 mode, no AR(1) trend) and a fixed-bimodal static mixture with NO switching (modes present but no temporal transitions / flat AR(1)). Earns place: separates switching from static-multimodal and flags pre-tipping with mean unchanged.
- **Risk/confound:** Mode count is sensitive to embedding + cluster-K selection (use gap statistic / BIC). Critical-slowing AR(1) trend false-trips on any slow non-critical drift in variance - must surrogate-calibrate against a stationary-with-drift null (the project's standard honest-bound move).

### [MEDIUM] Coarsening dynamics: domain-growth exponent + dynamic scaling collapse
- **Discriminator:** Track S(k) peak position k_peak(t) over time, L(t)=2pi/k_peak, fit L(t)~t^n. Discriminator = the GROWTH EXPONENT n (1/2 non-conserved, 1/3 conserved/LSW, ->0 arrested) and the QUALITY of dynamic-scaling collapse S(k,t)=L^d F(kL). This is explicitly the time-derivative structure of the scale the current S(k) lens already measures statically - it cannot be a shared absolute because two systems with identical instantaneous L differ in n.
- **Substrate:** continuous 2D field / binarized state field; particle phase domains (time series of frames)
- **Validation:** Positive control: Allen-Cahn (n~1/2) vs Cahn-Hilliard (n~1/3) coarsening; arrested/pinned spinodal (n->0). Null: a STATIC patterned field replayed (L constant -> n=0 but no collapse-over-time because no growth) and pure noise (no peak to track). Earns place: separates universality classes and detects glassy arrest that any single frame conflates.
- **Risk/confound:** Needs many frames spanning a scaling window; transients and finite-box saturation truncate the fit. Confound: distinguishing genuine arrest (n->0) from a system that simply finished coarsening (hit box size) - check k_peak vs box-limited k_min.

### [MEDIUM] Anomalous transport / diffusion class (MSD exponent, Levy, CTRW aging)
- **Discriminator:** Per-trajectory MSD exponent beta (sub/normal/super/ballistic) AND the EAMSD-vs-TAMSD gap (ergodicity breaking / aging) AND step-length tail index (Levy alpha<2) + waiting-time tail (CTRW). Discriminator = the EXPONENT beta and the ergodicity-breaking parameter, not the diffusion magnitude/coefficient (a shared absolute - a fast Brownian and a slow Brownian are both beta=1).
- **Substrate:** particle position trajectories; tracked field features
- **Validation:** Positive control: fractional Brownian motion (beta!=1), a Levy-flight searcher (power-law steps, alpha<2), and a CTRW with heavy waiting times (EAMSD!=TAMSD, aging). Null: ordinary Brownian (beta=1, EAMSD=TAMSD, Gaussian steps). Earns place: separates Levy vs Brownian searcher and sub- vs normal-diffusion (glassy caging) which share net displacement statistics.
- **Risk/confound:** Short/ few trajectories make tail fits unstable; finite trajectory length biases TAMSD. Overlaps the glassy axis (caging=subdiffusion) - keep MSD as the transport axis and chi_4 as the spatial-heterogeneity axis to avoid double-counting.

### [MEDIUM] Synchronization texture (chimera / cluster sync / explosive sync)
- **Discriminator:** Beyond global R: compute the LOCAL order-parameter field R(x) and test for SPATIAL BIMODALITY (coherent + incoherent domains coexisting = chimera); cluster oscillators by phase/frequency to count synced groups; build PLV matrix + its community structure; sweep coupling for hysteresis (explosive sync). Discriminator = bimodality of the local-R distribution and group count, NOT the global R value (the exact scalar that conflates chimera with uniform-partial sync).
- **Substrate:** oscillator phases (2D lattice / network); particle velocity-derived phases
- **Validation:** Positive control: known chimera regime (nonlocally coupled phase oscillators) -> bimodal R(x); cluster-sync regime -> k>1 phase groups; explosive-sync model -> hysteresis loop on coupling sweep. Null: globally partial sync (uniform intermediate R(x), unimodal) at the SAME global R as the chimera - the killer test. Earns place: fires on chimera where global-R lens reads identical to boring partial sync.
- **Risk/confound:** Local-R window size sets sensitivity (too big -> washes out chimera, too small -> noise). Defining 'incoherent domain' needs a surrogate threshold. Explosive-sync hysteresis requires a controllable coupling sweep, which may not be available for passively-observed substrates.

### [MEDIUM] Chaos dimension / determinism (low-dim vs spatiotemporal chaos vs noise)
- **Discriminator:** Delay-embed (Takens) and estimate correlation dimension D2 (Grassberger-Procaccia, with SATURATION-vs-embedding-dim as the determinism test) + largest Lyapunov (Rosenstein) + 0-1 test K. For fields, test EXTENSIVITY: dimension density scaling with system size (extensive=spatiotemporal chaos, saturating=low-dim). Discriminator = D2 saturation and dimension-vs-L SCALING, not the breadth of the power spectrum (a shared absolute across chaos/turbulence/noise).
- **Substrate:** representative scalar/reduced time series via delay embedding; field reduced modes
- **Validation:** Positive control: Lorenz/Rossler (low D2 that saturates, lambda>0, K~1); coupled-map-lattice spatiotemporal chaos (dimension ~ L, extensive). Null: filtered Gaussian noise (D2 does NOT saturate / grows with embedding dim; surrogate-data IAAFT test rejects determinism). Earns place: splits the three broadband regimes the spectral lens lumps; revives the retired recurrence axis ONLY for this determinism job, via embedding (the documented correct fix), not surrogate rescue.
- **Risk/confound:** D2/Lyapunov are fragile on short, noisy, non-stationary series - the exact reason recurrence was retired. Must gate on series length and pass an IAAFT surrogate-nonlinearity test first; otherwise honest result may be 'cannot certify' (acceptable bound, low false-positive).

### [MEDIUM] Compressibility / algorithmic-grammatical structure (LZ + epsilon-machine)
- **Discriminator:** Lempel-Ziv complexity (normalized vs shuffle) + block-entropy convergence to separate truly-random from algorithmically-simple-but-aperiodic; then epsilon-machine causal-state Cmu (CSSR) for the minimal generating grammar. Discriminator = LOW compressed size / FINITE causal-state count DESPITE high permutation entropy - i.e. the gap between LZ-compressibility and permutation-entropy. Targets deterministic-aperiodic sources (rule-30/90) that the existing MPR permutation-window calls noise.
- **Substrate:** symbolized field (space-filling-curve raster) / symbolized order-parameter or event stream
- **Validation:** Positive control: rule-30 CA (high permutation entropy but LZ-compressible, finite-ish causal structure) and rule-90/Sierpinski (deterministic-aperiodic). Null: true iid random stream (LZ ~ shuffle, Cmu high/non-converging) and a periodic signal (trivially compressible - distinguished by Cmu LOW for periodic vs intermediate for complex). Earns place: lights up the deterministic-aperiodic corner that permutation entropy maps to the noise corner.
- **Risk/confound:** Symbolization choice (binarization threshold, raster curve) strongly affects LZ/Cmu and can manufacture or destroy structure; needs a fixed principled symbolization + surrogate. CSSR is finicky on history length. Overlap with existing statistical-complexity lens - must show it catches cases MPR misses, else it's a duplicate.

### [MEDIUM] Hyperuniformity + giant number fluctuations (number-variance scaling)
- **Discriminator:** Sub-box number-variance sigma^2_N(R) scaling exponent + small-k slope of S(k): S(k->0)->0 / sigma^2~R^(d-1) = hyperuniform (hidden order); a>0.5 / S(k->0) divergent = giant number fluctuations (active matter). Discriminator = the LONG-WAVELENGTH variance SCALING EXPONENT (a cheap extension reading S(k) at small k), explicitly NOT the principal-peak position the current lens uses. Two distinct opposite signatures from one estimator.
- **Substrate:** particle positions; thresholded field as point pattern
- **Validation:** Positive control: maximally-random-jammed / absorbing-state-critical packing (hyperuniform, alpha>0) and a dry active nematic (GNF, a~0.8-1). Null: Poisson point process (a=0.5, flat S(k->0)) - the central reference. Earns place: detects 'disordered-but-organized' hyperuniformity and active GNF, both invisible to peak-based S(k) and to clustering.
- **Risk/confound:** Small-k / large-R estimates are the noisiest part of S(k) and most finite-size-sensitive (this is a finite-size-scaling-adjacent measurement). Needs careful box-size range and edge correction; honest output may be a bound near the Poisson line rather than a sharp class.

### [MEDIUM] Cross-substrate joint signatures (systematic combination-cell battery)
- **Discriminator:** A FAMILY of joint estimators, each provably absent from both marginals: cross-spectrum S_AB(k) peak for reciprocal cross-diffusion patterning (Turing wavelength in cross-coherence while each auto-S(k) is flat); closed-loop bidirectional cross-TE (product, not either) for stigmergy/sensorimotor loops; cross-susceptibility cov(field-giant-component, agent-giant-component) for co-percolation; inter-layer degree correlation + supra-Laplacian gap for multiplex; field-induced effective graph (deposition-kernel x sensing-kernel overlap) run through graph_structure for field-mediated competition. Discriminator in every case = a CROSS term that is zero in both single-substrate marginals (the literal definition the project already validated with P36/P37).
- **Substrate:** paired substrates: positions+field, two networks, field+network, two fields, fast+slow
- **Validation:** This is a GRID, run cell by cell like Ring-1. Per cell: positive control = a model where the coupling is ON (e.g. cross-diffusion Turing with k_peak only in S_AB); null = the SAME model with the cross-coupling severed / one substrate kernel spatially randomized (marginal statistics preserved, cross term destroyed). Earns place per-cell only if cross-signature fires AND both marginals stay below their own bars - the honest bound posture from the Ring-1 closure doc. Surface 2-way cells first, mirror the ring1_combination_cells.md methodology.
- **Risk/confound:** Combinatorial blow-up - cannot build all cells; prioritize cells with a clean severable null and a real candidate substrate pair. Major confound: a cross-signature that is actually inherited from a shared driver (must condition / use the edge-shuffle or kernel-randomized surrogate). Some cells (sensorimotor loop, embodiment) need an intervention/perturbation channel that passive observation lacks -> those drop to low priority.

### [LOW] Cross-frequency / cross-scale coupling (phase-amplitude, bicoherence, cascade flux)
- **Discriminator:** Phase-amplitude coupling modulation index (Tort KL between low-freq phase and high-freq amplitude) + bicoherence (third-order spectrum) for quadratic phase coupling among frequency triads + scale-to-scale energy flux. Discriminator = the MULTIPLICATIVE cross-frequency dependence (PAC modulation index above a phase-shuffled surrogate), which is invariant to and invisible in the linear power spectrum (the shared absolute) - two signals with identical spectra, one with PAC one without, separate only here.
- **Substrate:** scalar/field time series with >=2 separated frequency bands; field for scale-to-scale flux
- **Validation:** Positive control: synthetic theta-gamma PAC signal and a turbulent cascade (scale-to-scale flux). Null: two independent oscillators summed (same two spectral peaks, NO PAC -> modulation index = surrogate level). Earns place: fires on PAC-present vs PAC-absent pairs with matched spectra.
- **Risk/confound:** PAC is notoriously prone to false positives from sharp edges / non-sinusoidal waveforms / harmonics that mimic coupling; requires waveform-aware surrogates and is hard to make clean. That fragility is why this is low priority despite real novelty.

### [LOW] Nonequilibrium current / entropy production (broken detailed balance)
- **Discriminator:** Probability-current loops in coarse-grained state space (antisymmetric part of the transition-rate matrix) / cycle affinities / entropy-production rate from forward-vs-reverse trajectory KL. Discriminator = nonzero CIRCULATING, divergence-free probability current = broken detailed balance, distinct from net advective vorticity (which the current lens has) - it catches abstract state-space and edge-flux currents with zero structural change.
- **Substrate:** any coarse-grained to a discrete state space; field via optical-flow mass flux
- **Validation:** Positive control: a driven ratchet / non-equilibrium Markov cycle with a known nonzero current at structural steady state. Null: an equilibrium system at the same stationary distribution (detailed balance -> zero current, symmetric forward/reverse statistics). Earns place: flags a dissipative steady state that snapshot + flat-spectrum + vorticity all call 'static'.
- **Risk/confound:** State-space binning dominates the estimate; coarse bins can manufacture or hide currents, and current estimation needs lots of transition samples. Time-irreversibility KL is data-hungry and biased on short series. Genuinely hard to make confound-free -> low priority pending a clean binning protocol.

### [LOW] Hierarchical / nested multi-scale structure
- **Discriminator:** Detect MULTIPLE separated peaks/plateaus in S(k) (a SET of characteristic scales) and run recursive/hierarchical community detection -> dendrogram DEPTH + modularity-at-each-level + multiscale-entropy across scales. Discriminator = the COUNT of distinct separated scales / dendrogram depth, not the single dominant scale (the shared absolute the current S(k) and single-level modularity report).
- **Substrate:** field (multi-peak S(k) / wavelet); network (recursive community)
- **Validation:** Positive control: a synthetic clusters-of-clusters point set (two well-separated scales) and a hierarchical stochastic block model (>=2 community levels). Null: single-scale pattern (one S(k) peak) and a flat single-level modular graph (dendrogram depth 1). Earns place: recovers >=2 levels where current lenses collapse to one.
- **Risk/confound:** Distinguishing genuinely separated scales from a single broad peak / a smooth crossover is ill-posed without clear scale separation (a finite-size-scaling-adjacent ambiguity). Dendrogram depth is sensitive to the community-detection resolution parameter. Modest novelty over existing modularity -> low priority.

### [LOW] Long-range temporal memory / non-Markovian (DFA Hurst, 1/f, history-order)
- **Discriminator:** DFA Hurst exponent H!=0.5 + 1/f^beta spectral slope (cross-checked beta=2H-1) + Markov-order selection / excess-entropy depth (how many past steps are predictive). Discriminator = the SCALING EXPONENT H and the history DEPTH, not the autocorrelation magnitude at lag-1 (a shared absolute). Partly corroborated by anomalous-diffusion beta.
- **Substrate:** any global order-parameter / event-count / field-mean time series
- **Validation:** Positive control: fractional Gaussian noise with H>0.5 (persistent) and a 1/f process; long-history accumulator. Null: white noise (H=0.5, beta=0, Markov-order 0) and AR(1) (short memory, H=0.5 at long scales). Earns place: detects long-range memory invisible to a snapshot and to lag-1-only summaries.
- **Risk/confound:** Heavy overlap with the existing spectral lens (1/f is a spectral feature) and with the anomalous-transport axis (H<->beta). Risk of being a near-duplicate; would need to show it catches memory cases the spectral peak-gate misses (broadband 1/f with no peak). Low priority for that reason.

### [LOW] Extreme-value class + extreme-clustering (GEV shape, extremal index)

Discriminator = the TAIL-CLASS xi and the extreme-CLUSTERING theta, distinct from the bulk power-law exponent the existing heavy-tail lens fits (a different moment of the distribution).
- **Substrate:** scalar/order-parameter maxima; event-size stream; field peak-intensity series
- **Validation:** Positive control: a Frechet-domain heavy-tailed driver (xi>0) with correlated bursts (theta<1) and a bounded/jamming process (xi<0). Null: iid exponential-tail series (xi=0, theta=1, isolated extremes). Earns place: separates bounded-vs-unbounded extremes and clustered-vs-isolated extremes, neither captured by the bulk-tail fit.
- **Risk/confound:** Block-maxima / POT need many extremes -> data-hungry; threshold/block-size choice is subjective. Overlaps the existing heavy-tail lens enough that incremental value is modest -> low priority unless a substrate specifically shows extreme-clustering the bulk fit misses.

### [LOW] Ageing / two-time non-stationary relaxation (FDT violation)
- **Discriminator:** Two-time correlation C(t_w, t_w+tau) at several waiting times; ageing = FAILURE to collapse under tau alone but collapse under tau/t_w; + FDT-violation ratio (effective temperature != bath). Discriminator = the t_w-DEPENDENCE of the relaxation timescale, the exact structure stationary analysis assumes away - not the one-time autocorrelation decay (a shared absolute).
- **Substrate:** glassy/coarsening field; particle self-overlap; order-parameter autocorrelation
- **Validation:** Positive control: a coarsening or glassy model showing C depending on t_w and tau/t_w collapse. Null: an equilibrium stationary process (C depends on tau only, collapses trivially; FDT holds). Earns place: detects never-completing aging relaxation that any single-time correlation calls ordinary.
- **Risk/confound:** Requires many waiting times and long runs (expensive); FDT-violation needs a conjugate-field response measurement that passive observation often lacks. Strong overlap with glassy-dynamics axis -> fold in there if built. Low priority / hard validatability.
