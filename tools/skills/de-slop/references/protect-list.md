# Protect list

The floor strips general AI tells. This file lists what the floor must never
strip from Matt's writing. Before flagging or filing anything on Matt's byline,
load this first. If a floor flag collides with a protected item, surface the
collision and do not auto-edit.

A de-slop pass at full strength on everything sands a real writer down to the
same flat statistical profile it is meant to fix. This list is what keeps the
voice intact.

## Known protections (confirmed from working together)

- **Concision over completeness.** Matt cuts; do not re-add hedges, caveats, or
  balance he removed. A short direct claim is a choice, not an omission.
- **Direct verdicts.** Blunt assessments ("this slide is doing nothing") stay
  blunt. Do not soften into consultant diplomacy.
- **Domain vocabulary at full strength.** Clinical research operations terms
  (activation, accrual, coverage analysis, feasibility, NORA, SoA) are precise
  language, not jargon to simplify. Same for governance terms in the RWJBH
  work. Never "plain-language" a term of art.
- **Repetition of the right word.** In specs and governance documents, the same
  term repeats on purpose (a defined term stays the defined term). Never
  synonym-cycle a defined term for variety; that changes meaning.
- **Numbered rules and versioned artifacts.** "Rule 8", "v1.3.2",
  "Tranche 2" style references are identifiers that carry meaning. Leave
  exactly as written.
- **Scanner flags on protected moves.** `scripts/slop_scan.py` counts; it
  does not know this list. It will flag a rhetorical question, a "Y, not X."
  verdict, or a deliberate fragment as a candidate. On a byline, check each
  flag here before acting. At dose, the flag is noise.

## Voice-skill signatures (canonical: matt-voice/references/tone-essence.md)

These floor flags are Matt's signatures on his byline. Protected at the stated
dose; beyond the dose, or stripped of their function, flag as usual. All doses:
one or two signature moves per piece total, never as tics.

- **Rhetorical question + terse answer + qualifier.** "Has anything ever
  worked as I had planned? Probably not. And if so, it was probably built on
  the failures of many preceding attempts." The floor's rhetorical-setup rule
  does not apply to this move at dose.
- **Confident hedge as understatement.** "Probably not." "Likely." "What
  appears to be." Protected when the hedge fronts a position. Defensive
  hedge-stacks remain slop.
- **Deliberate fragments inside the question-answer move.** Protected there
  only. Not a general fragment license.
- **"I used to think X, but now Y."** Protected only with a stated, specific
  cause for the update. Without the cause, treat as binary-contrast slop.
- **Self-aware admission.** "I am not quite sure how to explain this yet."
  "The evidence here is thinner than I would like." Protected when true and
  relevant; the floor's uncertainty-stripping does not apply.
- **Personal-to-universal pivot, mentor references, self-disclosure +
  professional mask, substance + aesthetics paired.** No floor collision;
  listed so nothing "cleans" them into neutrality.

## Stricter-than-floor settings (the floor must not relax these)

- Binary contrast on client deliverables: zero, not "max one". FATAL.
- Em dashes inside sentences: zero everywhere, not just deliverables.
- Tricolons on deliverables: Tier 1, not the floor's default Tier 2 handling.
