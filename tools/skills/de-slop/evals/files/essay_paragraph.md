Has any activation plan I have written survived contact with the first site? Probably not. And when one did, it was usually because the coordinator on the other end had quietly rebuilt it around what her regulatory office would actually sign. That is the part of research operations the process diagrams never show. Accrual is a number the sponsor watches. Activation is a number the site lives inside — every week a study sits in coverage analysis is a week the coordinator explains to an investigator why the patient in clinic yesterday could not be screened. I used to think the fix was a better intake form, but after watching three health systems leverage the same form to three different outcomes, I now think the form is the least of it. The order in which the budget, the coverage analysis, and the IRB submission move is what decides the timeline, and that order is set by whoever in the office has the most patience, not by the SOP. The evidence here is thinner than I would like. It is also the most consistent thing I have seen in ten years of this work.
