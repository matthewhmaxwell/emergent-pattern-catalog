Here's the thing about clinical trial activation: nobody talks about it. 🚀

Everyone wants to talk about AI in healthcare. Cutting-edge models. Robust pipelines. Seamless integration. But the honest answer is that most sites still take 6+ months to open a study, and no amount of AI is going to fix a broken intake process.

I've spent the last decade in research operations, and here's what I've learned:

✅ Activation isn't a technology problem. It's a people problem.
✅ The best sites don't have better tools. They have better handoffs.
✅ Speed comes from clarity: clear roles, clear timelines, clear ownership.

It's not just about moving faster. It's about building trust with sponsors, investigators, and patients alike.

The future of clinical research is bright. But only if we genuinely fix the fundamentals first.
