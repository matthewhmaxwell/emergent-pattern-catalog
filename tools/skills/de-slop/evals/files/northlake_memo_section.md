# Integrating Research Operations Across the Northlake–Carver Affiliation

The affiliation between Northlake Health and Carver University represents a pivotal moment in the evolution of the region's research enterprise. It isn't a technology problem—it's a governance problem. Today, 14 clinical sites operate under three separate coverage-analysis workflows, ensuring that study activation timelines remain inconsistent across the enterprise. Preliminary data from the Q2 2026 baseline (excluding oncology, which reports through a separate committee) shows that 62% of interventional trials took longer than 120 days to activate, and the enterprise spent roughly $2.3M on duplicated feasibility work in FY2025.

A unified research operations office would streamline activation, foster collaboration, and ensure alignment across both institutions. By leveraging Microsoft Teams and the existing clinical trial management system, the office can establish a single robust intake process. The result is faster decisions, fewer handoffs, and a clear, consistent, and repeatable path from protocol receipt to first patient in.

Key considerations:

- **Governance:** which establishes a joint operations council, enabling shared accountability
- **Infrastructure:** which consolidates the three workflows into one, enabling faster activation
- **Culture:** which builds trust between the institutions, enabling long-term collaboration

This is not about consolidation for its own sake. It's about creating the conditions for research to thrive. Aligned governance. Shared infrastructure. Unified science.
