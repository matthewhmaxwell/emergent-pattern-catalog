RULE (plain words):
World: a 64x64 square lattice with wrap-around edges; each site holds a continuous mass (mean 1 per site). Rules, repeated: (1) pick a random site and one of the four directions at random; (2) the site keeps 40% of its mass plus a random share of the rest and sends the remainder one step in that direction; (3) the receiving site pools it with its own mass, keeps 60% plus a random share of the rest, and passes the remainder one step to its left (a 90-degree counter-clockwise turn), so mass moves along L-shaped paths that always turn the same way. Rates are identical for all four directions and total mass is conserved. Measure steady-state density-density correlations and long-wavelength fluctuations.
