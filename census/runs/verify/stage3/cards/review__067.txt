First-pass text (excerpt): **Verdict: KNOWN.** This is the Nowak–May spatial Prisoner's Dilemma with a small amount of strategy-mutation noise. Nothing in what was observed needs a new explanation, and the "domain coarsening" signal is most likely the system running down into a near-absorbing, cooperator-dominated state rather than real emergent coarsening.

**Why it's known**
- **The core model is textbook.** Payoffs R=1, P=0, S=0, T=b with b between 1 and 2, on a lattice with deterministic copy-the-best-neighbour updating, is the Nowak–May parameterization of the spatial PD. The original authors studied PD on a two dimensional array with synchronous updating and explored the asymptotic behavior for various values of the parameter b.
- **Noise toward cooperation is already covered.** Mukherji et al. tested perturbations including random introduction of cooperators and defectors at any site and spontaneous conversion of cooperators into defectors with some probability. Nowak et al. replied stating that, their results are robust with respect to these modifications. The one-way defector-to-cooperator flip here is just the cooperator half of "random introduction of cooperators and defectors".
- **The exact payoff and this noise direction appear in Helbing & Yu.** Their imitation-plus-noise runs use T=1.3, R=1, P=0.1, S=0, and their figures mark each defector who became a cooperator as a separate category.
