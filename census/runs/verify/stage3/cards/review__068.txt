**What these rules force (worked out by hand, not simulated)**
- Type 0 is the empty state. Nothing in the rules ever turns a type-2 cell into anything else, and nothing ever creates an empty cell. Rule (1) only removes empties, and moves just relocate them. So the drop to **n_types = 2** almost certainly means the empty cells disappeared, not type 2.
- Rule (1) on its own is known threshold growth, or bootstrap percolation: occupied sites stay occupied, and empty sites become occupied once three or more neighbours are occupied. Here the threshold is 3 on the 8-neighbour lattice, and only type-1 cells count.
- Once the empties are gone, no Schelling move is possible, so the system freezes.
