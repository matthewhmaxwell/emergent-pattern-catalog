**1. Model family**
This is a Nowak–May-type spatial evolutionary game. It is a deterministic, synchronous cellular automaton with imitate-the-best updating over the 8 neighbours plus self. It has unconditional-strategy players and a mobility step added.

- Nowak & May's original system is described as a deterministic spatial game where each site is taken by its original owner or a neighbour, whichever scores highest, and which can generate chaotically changing spatial patterns.
- A review of the Nowak–May model describes each player imitating the strategy of the neighbouring players (including herself) who scored the highest payoff.

**2. The payoffs are not a Prisoner's Dilemma**
