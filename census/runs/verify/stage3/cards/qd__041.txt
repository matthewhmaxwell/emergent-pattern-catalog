**Why it's degenerate**
1. **No feedback through the field.** No rule in this class ever adds anything to field 0. Diffusion only flattens the field, so it is either flat from the start or soon becomes flat. Either way, "turn up the gradient" ends up doing nothing, and the agents are just persistent random walkers (speed 0.3, heading noise 0.3 rad).
2. **No feedback through the cells.** Agents change cells but never read them, and cells have no update rule of their own. Turing-universality results for generalised Langton's ants specifically exclude this case: any Turmite, except for those whose rule does not depend on the symbol, can simulate any Turing Machine. These agents never read the symbol, which is exactly the excluded trivial case.
3. **The lattice stays random.** With 2 cell types, each cell just records whether it has been visited an odd or even number of times. That is the same as the classic picture where the visited sites turn alternately black and white depending on whether they are visited an odd or even number of times.
