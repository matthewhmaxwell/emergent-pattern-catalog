**1. Model family**
- The system is a spatial evolutionary Prisoner's Dilemma on a Moore-neighbourhood lattice with periodic boundaries. Each cell plays all 8 neighbours plus itself, then copies the best scorer. A weak random "copy a neighbour" mutation is added.
- Nowak and May introduced the base model. Individuals on a lattice play with their neighbours and with themselves. Between rounds each adopts the strategy with the highest payoff among its neighbours, itself included.
- Your payoffs are R=1, T=1.5, P=0, S=-0.5. That is a strict PD, since T>R>P>S.
