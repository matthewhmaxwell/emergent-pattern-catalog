**1. Model family**
- Rule (1) is the multi-state voter model. Each site copies a random neighbour on a lattice, here with Moore neighbourhood, 4 states and asynchronous-style updating at rate 0.1.
- The other "same class" programs (ALIGN, MAJ, WRITECELL) appear to reduce to copy-a-neighbour or majority dynamics on a lattice. I could not verify what those operators do, so this is an inference.
- Rule (2) is a one-way, field-triggered conversion of type 0 into type 2. This is like a state-selective bias or sink.
- I could not tell whether field 0 is ever seeded above 0.5. The run ends with 3 types, which fits either random extinction of one type or the type-0 sink.

**2. The behaviour and its sources**
- **Behaviour:** the 2D voter model coarsens, but with no surface tension. Interface density falls only as 1/ln t. Clusters of all sizes coexist, and a correlation length grows roughly as √t. This explains why the run passes the coarsening test but doesn't look like a curvature-driven (Ising/Potts) example. It has many small domains left (g_n_domains is high), a modest correlation length, and a persistent mixture of types.
