**1. Model family.** This is the 2-D cyclic cellular automaton (CCA), the Griffeath/Fisch–Gravner–Griffeath excitable-medium family. The rule is range 1, Moore neighbourhood, threshold 3, 4 states. In the R/T/C/N notation that is 1/3/4/M, which Griffeath's rule collections call "Perfect". The listed variants are the same lattice rule with 400 self-propelled agents added (kA = 1, 2 or 4). In every program shown, the agents are never given a rule that changes cells. The cell dynamics are therefore a stand-alone CCA, and the agents cannot create the standout features.
