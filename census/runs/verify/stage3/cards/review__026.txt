**How each observed feature maps onto existing work:**

1. **Segregation and coarsening with vacancies.** The noisy Schelling model with vacancies has a mapped phase diagram with three phases: frozen, segregated and mixed. In the segregated phase, coarsening is measured as a power-law decay with an exponent α ≃ 0.5 in the interface density.
2. **Swap and copy as noise on top of Schelling moves.** This exact pairing has been studied. There are rigorous hydrodynamic-limit results for the Schelling model with spontaneous Glauber and Kawasaki dynamics, which predict a mixed, a segregated and a metastable segregation phase. Your SWAP rule plays the Kawasaki role (neighbours exchange places) and COPY plays the Glauber role (a cell's type gets replaced). A swap rate of 1 just means strong noise.
