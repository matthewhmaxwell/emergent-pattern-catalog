**Correction to the first pass.** The first pass says the agents move through a "quenched random-torque landscape". That doesn't fit the rules for the main program. With kC=1 there is only one cell type, and from the description I take that to be type 0, which is even. So TURNCELL makes every agent turn left on every step. Nothing about it is random or depends on the landscape. Every agent is simply a noisy circle swimmer, and they all turn the same way. That is the standard chiral active particle. In the absence of fluctuations, chiral active particles (CAPs) typically move along circles (2D). The same point applies to the two kA=4 variants, since they also have kC=1. For background, see Löwen, Chirality in microswimmer motion: From circle swimmers to active turbulence, and van Teeffelen & Löwen, Dynamics of a Brownian circle swimmer, Physical Review E 78, 020101 (2008).
