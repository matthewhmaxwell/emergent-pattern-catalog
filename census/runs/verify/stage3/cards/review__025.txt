**Why the fingerprint doesn't look like textbook Schelling.** Rule (1) is a majority vote applied to every cell at once, every step, with probability 1. On a Moore (8-neighbour) lattice this rule settles down by itself: Moore-neighbourhood majority-vote CAs are Class 1, converging to a stable pattern, and converge to unanimity when one type starts with a large enough majority. Majority rule is a well-known way to model this: for dimensions above 1, majority rule is implemented as a zero-temperature spin-flip kinetic Ising model. For random-sequential majority rule, the model with an odd neighbourhood size is conjectured to cluster in any dimension. Moore (1997) studies the synchronous majority-vote CA directly and ties it to zero-temperature single spin-flip Ising dynamics.
