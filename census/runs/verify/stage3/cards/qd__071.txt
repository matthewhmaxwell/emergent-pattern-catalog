First-pass text (excerpt): **Verdict: KNOWN.** The behaviour is Schelling-type segregation with vacancies.

**1. Model family**
- The cell rules are a Schelling-type relocation model. Cells sit on a Moore-neighbourhood torus with vacancies. An occupied cell whose same-type share among occupied neighbours falls below a threshold (0.2) jumps to a random empty site. The literature calls this a "tolerance" or "acceptance" threshold. It is the minimal fraction of like neighbours below which agents consider moving out.
- Two things differ from the textbook version. There are 4 types instead of 2, and moves go to a random empty cell instead of a satisfying one. Random-vacancy relocation is a standard variant.
- The chemical part is a Gray–Scott-like autocatalytic step, u + 2v → 3v. One molecule of u and two of v become three of v. There is no feed or kill term here. Type-2 cells act as a source of v.

**2. The specific behaviour**
- In this rule set the cells never read the field. The only coupling is one-way: type-2 cells emit into field 1. The segregation dynamics are therefore identical to a plain Schelling model.
- The field is a passive bystander. I found no literature on this exact cell-to-field coupling, and I judge it irrelevant to the segregation result.
