**1. Model family**
- Rule (1), where each cell copies a random neighbour, is the multi-state voter model on a 2D lattice. It uses an 8-neighbour (Moore) neighbourhood, asynchronous-like updating with p = 0.2, and 4 states.
- Rule (2) makes type-0 cells emit a diffusing scalar. The description gives no decay term and no feedback from the field to the cells.
- That makes the coupling one-way, so the cell layer is a pure voter model. The field is the time-integrated type-0 source smoothed by diffusion.
- The two sibling programs use majority or Schelling-threshold (C.SCHELL, th=0.2) rules in place of, or alongside, copying. They belong to the same family of local-consensus or segregation dynamics that coarsen into domains.
