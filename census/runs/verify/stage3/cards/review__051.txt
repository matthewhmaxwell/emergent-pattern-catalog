**What the rule actually is.** Type 0 ("empty") is one of the four cyclic states, so the conversion rule turns empty cells into type 1 and type 3 into empty. That makes it a standard 4-state cyclic cellular automaton (CCA) with threshold 1 on the 8-cell neighbourhood. It also means vacancies are *not* conserved, which contradicts the first pass's "conserved vacancies" claim. The Schelling step only moves cells into whichever cells happen to be type 0 at that moment, and those cells are themselves part of the cycle.

**The documented phase this matches.** For the CCA with an 8-cell, range-one neighbourhood, T=1 and T=2 are both locally periodic, but T=2 produces spiral waves, while T=1 "quenches" incoherent local oscillations. Shalizi & Shalizi illustrate exactly this setup: the number of colors is fixed to 4, the rule uses a range-one box-shaped neighborhood, and the initial conditions are a uniform random mixture. They describe a slight departure from uniform randomness, in the shape of connected twisting single-color bands. The CA as a whole oscillates with period 4, each cell cycling through the colors.
