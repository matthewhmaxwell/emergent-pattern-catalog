- **Outside type-flipping noise in Schelling is a studied topic.** Hazan & Randon-Furling add agents that change spontaneously from one of the two types of agents to the other. That amounts to imposing a noise or a perturbation in the form of a spatially-fixed random background within the Schelling dynamics, and it keeps the standard model's vacancies. The result: switching agents produce "intermediate patterns" such as "mixture of patches" and "fuzzy interfaces," and increasing [their fraction] expands the mixed phase while shrinking the segregated region.
